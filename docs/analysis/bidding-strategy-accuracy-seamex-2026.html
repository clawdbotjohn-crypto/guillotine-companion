02 · Stability

The winner changes when extremes move.

These filters were declared before inspecting winners and applied independently. They are sensitivity checks, not permission to delete inconvenient evidence.

Token threshold

Low intent means bid ≤ max($1, 1% of original FAAB) = $5. Tokens remain in the all-bid view and are separated here.

Three outlier lenses

Ratio-gap flagged 1 isolated top; MAD flagged 3; IQR flagged 4. Different robust rules produce different leaders.

Honest conclusion

Aggressive is the sample winner. There is no robust winner. Treat this as a hypothesis, not a default-setting result.

04 · How to read the math

Measures, assumptions, and guardrails.

All formulas are evaluated on dollars unless marked normalized. Predictions are intrinsic strategy values; manager forecasts are capped at reconstructed pre-bid FAAB.

MAE

MAE = (1/n) Σ |predicted − actual|

Average dollar miss. Easy to interpret; extreme bids still matter.

Median absolute error

Median AE = median(|predicted − actual|)

The typical dollar miss; less sensitive to a small number of extremes.

Signed bias

Bias = (1/n) Σ (predicted − actual)

Negative means systematic underprediction; positive means overprediction.

Spearman rank correlation

ρ = Pearson correlation(rank(actual), rank(predicted))

Tests whether expensive targets rank above cheap ones. Ties receive average ranks.

R² diagnostic

R² = 1 − SSE / SST

SSE = Σ(actual−predicted)²; SST = Σ(actual−mean(actual))². R² can be negative when a formula is worse than predicting the sample mean. That is valid here: intrinsic valuations were not fitted regressions, so R² is often unsuitable as a headline ranking.

Normalized FAAB error

mean(|predicted/FAAB − actual/FAAB|)

Reported against original $500 and reconstructed pre-bid manager FAAB. It compares budget share, not willingness.

Cluster-bootstrap interval

2,000 resamples of player × processing-batch clusters

Fixed seed 20260927; percentile 2.5%–97.5%. Keeps correlated win/loss claims together. It does not repair snapshot-history error.

Coverage

predicted ∈ [lowest serious loss, winning bid]

Computed on 19 competitive player/batch clusters. It asks whether a strategy falls inside observed serious bidding range.

Outlier rules

ratio ≥ 2× + $50 gap; MAD > median + 3×1.4826×MAD; IQR > Q3 + 1.5×IQR

MAD/IQR also require a $25 gap; minimum cluster sizes are 3 and 4. Flags are independent and never silently deleted.

Within tolerance

|predicted − actual| ≤ max($5, 20% of actual)

A practical accuracy band shown alongside continuous error measures.
